Open Data

Open Data & Downloads

The Agri-Energy Desk publishes its project register as open, machine-readable data. Download the full dataset, or explore the regional aggregates below.

Available datasets

Both files cover every published project on the platform and are regenerated on each request, so a download is always current. Unpublished and draft records are never included.

📊

Projects dataset (CSV)

CSV

Every published agri-energy project with region, district, value chain, status, funding partner, budget, beneficiary count and coordinates.

Download CSV — Projects dataset (CSV)
🗺️

Projects dataset (GeoJSON)

GeoJSON

The same published projects as a GeoJSON FeatureCollection of point features — ready to drop into QGIS, Leaflet, Mapbox or kepler.gl.

Download GeoJSON — Projects dataset (GeoJSON)

Projects by region

Aggregated counts, committed budget and reported beneficiaries for published projects, grouped by region. These are the same figures that shade the project map.

📈

No regional data yet

Regional aggregates will appear once projects with a region are published.

Budget figures are summed across the currencies recorded on each project and are indicative only. Beneficiary counts are as reported by implementing partners.

Licence & attribution

These datasets are published as open data for research, planning, journalism and programme design. You are free to reuse and redistribute them, provided you attribute the Agri-Energy Desk and state the date of download.

If you spot an error or have data to contribute, please get in touch.

Programmatic access

Everything on this platform is served by a public read-only REST API. The open-data endpoints are:

  • GET /api/v1/open-data/projects.csv
  • GET /api/v1/open-data/projects.geojson
  • GET /api/v1/open-data/regions

For bulk access, higher rate limits or a custom extract, contact the Desk team.
