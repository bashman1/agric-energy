Applications open: Improved Cookstoves support for Vegetables cooperatives

Improved Cookstoves progress in Gulu: how it is reshaping the Vegetables value chain across Northern Uganda.

<p>Improved Cookstoves is gaining momentum across Northern Uganda, where farmers in the Vegetables value chain are adopting clean-energy solutions to cut costs and boost productivity.</p><p>In Gulu, cooperatives report improved yields and reduced post-harvest losses after deploying Improved Cookstoves systems. Development partners and the National Renewable Energy Platform (NREP) continue to coordinate financing, technical standards and monitoring to scale what works.</p><p>"This is exactly the kind of productive-use investment Uganda's agri-energy sector needs," said a programme coordinator. Further rollouts are planned for neighbouring districts over the coming seasons.</p>
