About Agri-Energy

Understanding how energy supports agriculture in Uganda — and why a national digital platform matters.

What is Agri-Energy?

Agri-energy refers to the application of energy — particularly renewable energy — to agricultural production, processing, storage, and value addition. It encompasses technologies and practices that use solar, biogas, and other clean energy sources to power irrigation systems, food processing equipment, cold chain infrastructure, and farm machinery.

In Uganda, agri-energy plays a critical role in transforming subsistence agriculture into productive commercial activity. By replacing diesel fuel and removing dependence on rain-fed production, agri-energy technologies help farmers achieve higher yields, reduce losses, extend marketing windows, and ultimately increase their incomes.

The Agri-Energy Desk documents, organises, and makes accessible the full landscape of agri-energy technologies, projects, and knowledge resources from across Uganda.

💧
Solar-Powered Irrigation

Replacing diesel pumps and rain dependence with reliable solar water pumping systems.

🧊
Cold Chain & Storage

Solar cold rooms and refrigeration to reduce post-harvest losses in dairy, horticulture, and fish.

⚙️
Agro-Processing

Solar-powered milling, drying, and processing equipment to add value to agricultural produce.

🔥
Clean Cooking

Biogas digesters and improved biomass systems reducing firewood use and indoor air pollution.

Why the Agri-Energy Desk matters

📦

Fragmented Information

Information on agri-energy interventions in Uganda is scattered across organisations, reports, and websites — difficult for any stakeholder to navigate effectively.

🔍

Limited Visibility

There is no single space where users can identify who is implementing what, where, using which technologies, and for which beneficiary groups.

📈

Evidence Gap

A growing need for evidence to support planning, investment, and coordination requires structured data and curated knowledge resources.

How to Use This Platform

The Agri-Energy Desk is organised into several interconnected modules, each serving a specific purpose.

🏗️
Project Database

Search and filter agri-energy projects by district, technology, value chain, partner, and status.

⚡
Technologies Library

Deep-dive reference pages on specific agri-energy technologies including applications, benefits, and maintenance.

📚
Thematic Hub

Browse agri-energy knowledge organised by cross-cutting themes such as financing, gender, and climate.

🤝
Partners Directory

Find organisations active in agri-energy across Uganda — government, NGOs, private sector, and research.

📄
Resources Library

Access reports, guides, policy briefs, case studies, and datasets relevant to agri-energy.

🗺️
Geographic Map

Explore where agri-energy projects are located across Uganda's districts and regions.
