## 1. Objectives

On completion, NREP administrators will be able to, without assistance:

1. Manage content of every type through its full lifecycle.
2. Operate the editorial review workflow and apply consistent review standards.
3. Administer user accounts, approvals and roles.
4. Generate, interpret and distribute administrative reports.
5. Configure the homepage, featured content and taxonomies.
6. Use the audit trail to answer accountability questions.
7. Recognise the boundary between routine administration and issues requiring technical
   support, and know how to escalate.

## 3. Administrator programme (two days)

### Day 1 — Content and workflow

| Module | Duration | Content | Practical exercise |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Orientation | 45 min | Platform purpose; who sees what; roles and permissions; signing in; touring the admin portal | Sign in and locate every admin screen |
| 2. Content management | 2 hrs | List screens, filters; creating and editing every content type; slugs and why they matter; relationships; publishing and featuring; media upload | Create a project with technologies, partners and a location; publish it; verify it publicly; unpublish it |
| 3. Editorial review | 1.5 hrs | The review queue; reading a submission critically; approving; rejecting with useful notes; review standards and what to check before approving | Review and approve one submission; reject another with notes and confirm the contributor sees them |
| 4. Enquiries and newsletter | 1 hr | The enquiry inbox and its lifecycle; subscriber management; handling personal data responsibly | Process an enquiry from new to archived |

### Day 2 — Administration and reporting

| Module | Duration | Content | Practical exercise |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5. User administration | 1.5 hrs | Approving registrations; verifying an organisation is genuine; assigning roles; suspending accounts; least privilege | Approve a test registration; assign a role; suspend and restore |
| 6. Reporting | 2 hrs | The eight reports and what each answers; setting reporting periods; reading each section; downloading PDF and Excel; the limits of the figures (self-reported budgets; utilisation reflects editorial activity, not readership) | Generate the Project Portfolio and User Engagement reports for a set period and export both formats |
| 7. Configuration and audit | 1 hr | Homepage and featured content; taxonomies and the effect of renaming a term; reading and filtering the audit trail | Change a featured record; trace the day's own actions in the audit trail |
| 8. Operations and support | 1 hr | Routine daily/weekly/monthly tasks; recognising a fault; what to include in a support request; escalation path; warranty scope | Draft a support request for a simulated fault |
| 9. Assessment and close | 45 min | Competency assessment (§6); questions; handover of materials | |

**Delivery notes.** Hands-on throughout, on a staging environment with realistic content,
each participant on their own account. Every participant leaves with the
[Administrator Manual](09-administrator-manual.md).
