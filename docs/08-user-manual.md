# User Manual

**Deliverable:** ToR Deliverable 9 (part 1 of 2) — User manual
**Audience:** Public visitors and registered partner contributors

---

## 1. About the Agri-Energy Desk

The Agri-Energy Desk is Uganda's national gateway to information on the productive use of
renewable energy in agriculture. It brings together projects, technologies, thematic
knowledge, organisations, publications, policies, events and funding opportunities in one
place.

You do not need an account to browse, search or download anything from the public site.
An account is needed only to contribute content.

---

## 2. Getting around

The main navigation carries:

| Menu item | What you will find |
|---|---|
| **Projects** | Agri-Energy initiatives implemented across Uganda, filterable and mapped |
| **Technologies** | Renewable energy technologies for agricultural value chains |
| **Thematic** | Thematic areas, each gathering the projects, technologies and resources that belong to it |
| **Partners** | Profiles of government bodies, development partners, financiers, private sector actors, researchers and civil society organisations |
| **Resources** | The knowledge repository: research reports, technical manuals, policy documents, case studies and multimedia |
| **Updates** | A menu containing **Events**, **Opportunities** and **News** |
| **Map** | Geographic view of projects with regional statistics |
| **About** | Background on the platform and NREP |

The search box in the header searches everything at once. Type at least two characters and
suggestions appear; press Enter for full results.

## 3. Finding information

### 3.1 Browsing a directory

Each directory page (Projects, Technologies, Partners, Resources, Events, Opportunities)
opens with a filter bar. Filters combine — selecting a region and a value chain shows only
records matching both. Clearing a filter widens the results again. The URL updates as you
filter, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.

### 3.2 Reading a detail page

A detail page presents the full record: description, key facts, and links to related
records. From a project you can reach the technologies it uses and the partners
implementing it; from a technology you can reach the projects deploying it.

### 3.3 Using the map

The Map page plots geolocated projects. Click a marker for a summary and a link to the
full project. Regional shading reflects the number of projects, total reported budget and
beneficiaries in each region.

### 3.4 Searching

Search covers every published record. Results are ranked, with title matches ahead of
matches in the body text. Use the filters on the results page to narrow by content type.

## 4. Downloading information

### 4.1 Record factsheets

Every detail page carries a **Download** button offering two formats:

- **PDF factsheet** — a formatted, printable summary of the record, including its related
  records, with the source URL and generation date in the footer. Suitable for briefings
  and offline reference.
- **Excel workbook** — the same content as structured spreadsheet data, for reuse in your
  own analysis.

Both are generated on request and always reflect the current published version.

### 4.2 Open data

The **Data** page provides bulk datasets for analysts and integrators:

- Published projects as CSV
- Geolocated projects as GeoJSON, for use in any GIS tool
- Regional aggregate statistics

These require no account and no registration.

## 5. Staying informed

### 5.1 Newsletter

Enter your email in the newsletter form in the site footer or on the Newsletter page. You
will receive a confirmation email — your subscription is not active until you click the
link in it. Every newsletter contains an unsubscribe link, which takes effect immediately.

### 5.2 Contacting NREP

Use the **Contact** page to send an enquiry, correct information, or propose content. Your
message reaches the platform team's inbox and is tracked to a response.

## 6. Contributing as a partner organisation

### 6.1 Registering

1. Go to **Partner Portal** (top-right of the header) and choose **Register**.
2. Complete the organisation profile: legal name, type, registration details, coverage,
   sectors and value chains, and a contact person.
3. Submit the form.

Your account is created in **pending** status. You cannot sign in until an NREP
administrator approves it — this protects the integrity of the national directory.

Approval is not currently announced by email: try signing in after a few working days, or
ask NREP through the Contact page if you have heard nothing.

### 6.2 Signing in

Go to **Partner Portal → Login** and sign in with the email and password you registered.
Sessions last eight hours; after that you will be asked to sign in again.

### 6.3 Submitting content

From the partner dashboard, choose the type of record you wish to contribute (for example
a project or an event) and complete the form. Fields marked required must be filled.

**What happens next.** Your submission is saved with the status **pending** and is *not*
published. An NREP reviewer will:

- **Approve** it — the record is published and appears on the public site; or
- **Reject** it — the record is returned to you with reviewer notes explaining what needs
  to change.

You cannot publish content directly; this is by design and applies to every contributor.

### 6.4 Tracking your submissions

The partner dashboard lists everything you have submitted with its current status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Saved but not yet submitted for review |
| Pending | Submitted, waiting for a reviewer |
| In review | A reviewer is working on it |
| Published | Approved and live on the public site |
| Rejected | Returned to you — read the reviewer notes, revise and resubmit |

### 6.5 Uploading files

Where a form offers a file upload (for example a publication attached to a resource), use
it rather than pasting an external link where you can — files hosted on the platform stay
available even if your own website changes.

## 7. Accessibility

The platform targets WCAG 2.1 Level AA. It is fully keyboard-navigable, includes a skip
link to the main content, and works with screen readers. If you encounter an accessibility
barrier, please report it through the **Contact** page — the **Accessibility** page
explains our commitment and how to raise an issue.

---

## 8. Language

Use the language selector in the top bar to switch the interface between **English**,
**French** and **Kiswahili**. Content contributed by partners appears in the language it
was submitted in.

## 9. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| I cannot sign in after registering | Your account is awaiting administrator approval. Contact NREP through the Contact page if it has been more than a few working days. |
| I have forgotten my password | Self-service password reset is not yet available. Contact NREP through the Contact page and a reset will be arranged for you. |
| My submission has not appeared publicly | Check its status on your dashboard. Pending submissions await a reviewer; rejected ones carry notes explaining why. |
| A download does not open | Ensure your device has a PDF reader or spreadsheet application. Try again — documents are generated fresh on each request. |
| A page looks wrong or a link is broken | Report it through the Contact page, including the page address. |
| Search returns nothing | Try fewer or more general words. Only published content is searchable. |
